What is 16 Guti?

16 Guti (ষোল গুটি, “sixteen beads”) is a two-player strategy board game played across Bangladesh, India, Pakistan and the rest of South Asia. It goes by many names: Sholo Guti, 16 Goti, 16 Koti, 16 Khadi, 16 Sal, Sur Baggi, Bagh Bakri, Baghchal, Bara Tehni and, internationally, Sixteen Soldiers.

The rules are simple enough to learn in a minute, but the strategy runs deep, which is why many people compare it to checkers.

How to play

  • Each player starts with 16 beads on a lined board.
  • Players take turns moving one bead along the lines.
  • Jump over an opponent’s bead to capture it and remove it from the board.
  • Capture all of your opponent’s beads to win. Smart positioning is the key.
