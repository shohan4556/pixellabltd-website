About us

A lean studio in Dhaka, shipping real-time games

PixelLab Ltd. is a game studio in Dhaka, Bangladesh. We build real-time multiplayer Bengali board games and live social apps used by players across South Asia.

Our story

PixelLab Ltd. was founded in 2024 by Shohanur Rahaman. We’re a lean team that loves building, experimenting and shipping. Instead of portfolio pieces, we ship live products with real players and keep updating them.

Our games bring traditional South Asian board games like 16 Guti (Sholo Guti) online, so people can play the games they grew up with against friends and real opponents anywhere.

Our technology

Every PixelLab app runs on our own real-time social multiplayer SDK. The same engine runs every board in 16 Guti and every live room in Adda:

  • Server-authoritative: every game action is validated on the server, so results stay fair.
  • Queue-based matchmaking: players are paired into a synced room in seconds.
  • Reconnect and resync: lose signal mid-match and rejoin exactly where you left off.
  • Live streaming: streams hold up even on a weak connection.
