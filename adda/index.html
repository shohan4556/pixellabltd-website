Creators and fans, connected in real time

Adda Live brings live streams, a lifestyle content feed and direct creator connections into one app. We believe creators deserve better ways to turn their passion into a livelihood, and fans deserve real, direct ways to support them.

Features

Discover your feed

Fashion, beauty, food, travel, comedy and lifestyle videos, plus live streams from creators you follow.

Go live, anytime

Creators can go live for a hangout, talent showcase or Q&A. Join in and chat as it happens.

1:1 video calls

Talk directly with creators you follow on a private video call.

Personal video requests

Choose a creator and get a video made just for you or someone special.

Virtual gifts

Send gifts during live streams to celebrate a moment and support a creator.

Vetted creators

Every creator is verified before they can go live or accept requests. No fake profiles.

Safety

Creator verification is built into Adda Live from the start. Read our Child Safety Standards and Privacy Policy.
