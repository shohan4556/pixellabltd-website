Child Safety Standards

Effective Date: 12th August, 2026

This page applies to Adda - Live Stream & Chat ("Adda Live", "the Service"), developed and operated by PixelLab Ltd. ("we", "us", "our").

OUR COMMITMENT TO CHILD SAFETY

Adda Live prohibits Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) in any form. We do not allow content that sexualizes, exploits, or endangers minors on our platform. Creating a respectful and trustworthy environment is one of our top priorities, and we are committed to protecting our community through clear policies, robust tools, and continuous improvements so that users feel safe on Adda Live.

COMMUNITY GUIDELINES

To maintain a safe, healthy, and orderly Adda Live community, and to protect every user's rights, please adhere strictly to the standards on this page. Any violation may result in a warning, feature restrictions, account suspension, device blacklisting, fines, or a combination of these, depending on severity.

CHILD SAFETY POLICY

Adda Live has zero tolerance for any form of child abuse, exploitation, or sexual activity involving minors. Any user who violates this Child Safety Policy will be permanently banned and may also face legal action from law enforcement authorities.

AGE RESTRICTIONS

  • Users must be at least 18 years old to access the Adda Live platform.
  • Any attempt to falsify age to access the platform is strictly prohibited. Accounts will be suspended or deleted upon discovery.
  • Adda Live employs a combination of human moderation and intelligent monitoring systems to detect and prevent underage activity and any form of Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE). Attempts to bypass these safeguards are treated as a serious violation.

EXPLICITLY PROHIBITED ACTIVITIES

1. Sexualization of Minors and CSAE

  • Creation, sharing, or distribution of any CSAE content is strictly prohibited.
  • No fetishization of minors, including related discussions, suggestive performances, or provocative attire.
  • Depictions of child nudity or sexualization in any form, including drawings or animations, are not allowed.

2. Harmful or Dangerous Acts Involving Minors

  • No physical violence against minors.
  • No psychological abuse, coercion, or manipulation of minors.
  • No depictions or encouragement of dangerous activities involving minors.
  • No promotion or depiction of neglect, trafficking, or the sale of minors.

3. Minor Representation on the Platform

  • Minors are not allowed to appear in livestreams, videos, or any content on the platform.
  • No creation or management of accounts on behalf of minors.
  • No falsifying age to access the platform.

PRIVACY PROTECTION

Adda Live strictly prohibits any infringement of privacy or the distribution of private content without explicit consent. Private images, videos, or personal information must not be recorded, captured, sold, or shared without the subject's clear permission.

Technical measures against screen recording: to maximize user privacy, Adda Live has deployed multi-layer safeguards at both the client and server level to help detect and block screen recording and external capture during private interactions.

Enforcement: if any user or device is found to have recorded, shared, or distributed unauthorized private content, Adda Live will immediately remove the offending content, permanently ban the account, and blacklist the device where feasible to prevent future registration or login.

ACCOUNT SECURITY TIPS

  • Always protect your personal privacy. Do not display sensitive information or engage in activities that violate the platform's content guidelines during video calls.
  • Use a strong, unique password (12+ characters with letters, numbers, and symbols) and never reuse it across platforms.
  • Be cautious of phishing attempts — Adda Live will never ask for your password or request a transfer via email or private message.

Penalties: permanent account ban; permanent device ban; serious cases will be referred to law enforcement.

OTHER PROHIBITED CONDUCT

Rumors & False Information

Adda Live is committed to building a trustworthy environment. Deliberately spreading false information that misleads users or damages the platform's reputation is a serious violation.

Violation: fabricating or distributing unverified rumors; falsified screen recordings; maliciously misrepresenting others or platform policies; coordinated fake reports.
Penalties: permanent account ban; all associated devices blacklisted.

Adult Content & Body Exposure

Adda Live is a social and entertainment platform, not an adult-services site. No sexual or suggestive material is allowed.

Violation: live sexual acts; sexual solicitation; nudity or sexual content, including exposure of sexual organs; deliberate emphasis on intimate body parts.
Penalties: content removal; repeated violations lead to feature restrictions (live streaming, video chat) and ultimately account suspension.

Verification & Geolocation Evasion

Violation: use of VMs, emulators, VPNs, proxies, or other tools to evade face verification or IP/geolocation restrictions.
Penalties: account ban; device blacklisting; possible fine.

Multiple Accounts

Creating multiple accounts to boost metrics, evade sanctions, or farm gifts is not allowed.

Violation: repeated registration; switching accounts to avoid penalties; mass account farming.
Penalties: related new accounts banned for face verification.

Inappropriate Profile Images

Profiles must reflect the real user. Stolen, AI-generated, or any inappropriate image is not allowed.

Violation: using someone else's photo; frequently uploading vulgar or violent avatars; implying a false identity.
Penalties: profile image removal; repeated violations result in account suspension.

Other Misconduct

Including but not limited to:

  • Harassment, hate speech, and discrimination
  • Violence, gore, and animal abuse
  • Self-harm and suicide
  • Illegal transactions and prohibited items
  • Intellectual property and copyright violations
  • Spam, automation, and data scraping

Penalties: content removal; account ban or device ban for severe cases.

ENFORCEMENT & APPEALS

1. Monitoring: we employ automated detection and human review to identify and act on violations.

2. Sanctions: measures range from content takedown and feature restrictions to permanent account/device bans and, in the case of confirmed CSAE, referral to law enforcement and reporting to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) CyberTipline.

3. Appeals: if you believe a penalty was issued in error or wish to request leniency, you may submit an appeal through in-app customer support within 3 business days. Please include all relevant evidence and a clear explanation. Adda Live will review all appeals and issue a decision within 10 business days. Accounts banned for confirmed CSAE violations are not eligible for appeal.

UPDATES & RIGHT OF FINAL INTERPRETATION

Ongoing revisions: these standards are reviewed regularly to reflect legal changes, technological advances, and user feedback. PixelLab Ltd reserves the right to modify, add, or remove any provision at its discretion.

Notice period: changes take effect immediately upon posting. We recommend reviewing this page often. Continued use of Adda Live after updates constitutes acceptance of the new standards.

SAFETY GUIDE

Protect Yourself Online

  • Keep conversations on Adda Live — until you fully trust someone, keep calls and messages inside the app, and be cautious if they push to move to other platforms.
  • Protect your personal information — do not share your address, ID/passport numbers, verification codes/OTP, banking or crypto details, work location, daily routine, or travel plans with strangers.
  • Reject off-app transfers or gifts — decline any requests for money, gift exchanges, or cryptocurrency, and report such requests immediately.
  • Use a strong, unique password — at least 12 characters with letters, numbers, and symbols. Never reuse passwords across platforms.
  • Beware of phishing attempts — Adda Live will never ask for your password or money via email or private message. Always verify links before clicking.

Use Safety Tools

Report: tap the report icon on any profile, stream, message, or post, confirm you'd like to report it, and our team will review it promptly.

Prevent Image & Video Misuse

  • Adda Live uses multi-layer anti-recording technology to help block screen capture during live interactions.
  • If you suspect someone has recorded or shared your content without consent: end the interaction immediately, report the user via Adda Live, and contact local law enforcement if applicable.
  • Non-consensual sharing of intimate content is strictly prohibited and may be illegal.
