Child Safety Standards

Effective Date: 12th August, 2026

This page applies to Adda - Live Stream & Chat ("Adda Live", "the Service"), developed and operated by PixelLab Ltd. ("we", "us", "our").

Adda Live prohibits Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) in any form. We do not allow content that sexualizes, exploits, or endangers minors on our platform.

OUR COMMITMENT TO COMMUNITY SAFETY

At Adda Live, creating a respectful and trustworthy environment is one of our top priorities. We are committed to protecting our community through clear policies, robust tools, and continuous improvements that ensure users feel safe, valued, and free to connect authentically.

COMMUNITY GUIDELINES

Adda Live is a space where people can connect, communicate, and explore new friendships. To keep our platform positive and respectful, we've established clear Community Guidelines that outline the behaviors we expect and the ones we prohibit. These rules help foster a supportive and inclusive environment for all users, and we enforce them through proactive moderation and strict penalties for violations.

To maintain a safe, healthy, and orderly Adda Live community, and to protect every user's rights, please adhere strictly to the standards on this page. Any violation may incur warnings, feature restrictions, account suspension, device blacklisting, fines, or a combination thereof, depending on severity.

ADDA LIVE CHILD SAFETY POLICY

Adda Live has zero tolerance for any form of child abuse, exploitation, or sexual activity involving minors. Any user who violates this Child Safety Policy will be permanently banned and may also face legal action from law enforcement authorities.

Age Restrictions

  • Users must be at least 18 years old to access the Adda Live platform.
  • Any attempt to falsify age to access the platform is strictly prohibited. Accounts will be suspended or deleted upon discovery.
  • Adda Live employs a combination of human moderation and intelligent monitoring systems to detect and prevent underage activity and any form of Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE). Attempts to bypass detection will not be tolerated.

Explicitly Prohibited Activities

1. Sexualization of Minors and CSAE

  • Creation, sharing, or distribution of any CSAE content is strictly prohibited.
  • No fetishization of minors, including related discussions, suggestive performances, or provocative attire.
  • Depictions of child nudity or sexualization in any form, including drawings or animations, are not allowed.

2. Harmful or Dangerous Acts Involving Minors

  • No physical violence against minors.
  • No psychological abuse, coercion, or manipulation of minors.
  • No depictions or encouragement of dangerous activities involving minors.
  • No promotion or depiction of neglect, trafficking, or sale of minors.

3. Minor Representation on the Platform

  • Minors are not allowed to appear in livestreams, videos, or any content on the platform.
  • No creation or management of accounts on behalf of minors.
  • No falsifying age to access the platform.

Reporting Mechanism

  • Any incident involving child abuse will be reported to relevant protection agencies or law enforcement in accordance with applicable laws, including the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) CyberTipline.
  • Users can report suspicious activity via the in-app reporting tool or by emailing the official reporting address below.
  • Any user found to have committed crimes against children, including but not limited to sexual assault, physical abuse, neglect, or trafficking, will be permanently banned.

Penalties

Any violation of child safety policies will result in a permanent ban of the account and all associated devices. Adda Live will cooperate fully with law enforcement in severe cases.

PRIVACY PROTECTION

Adda Live strictly prohibits any infringement of privacy or the distribution of private content without explicit consent. Any private images, videos, or personal information must not be recorded, captured, sold, or shared without the subject's clear permission.

Technical Measures Against Screen Recording

To maximize user privacy protection, Adda Live has deployed multi-layer safeguards at both the client and server level to detect and block screen recording and external capture during private interactions. If a risk is detected, protective measures are applied immediately to help prevent unauthorized content acquisition.

Strict Enforcement & Penalties

If any user or device is found to have shared or distributed unauthorized recordings or private content, Adda Live will:

  • Immediately remove the offending content.
  • Permanently ban the account.
  • Blacklist the device to prevent future registration or login, where feasible.

ACCOUNT SECURITY TIPS

  • Always protect your personal privacy. Do not display sensitive information or engage in any activities that violate the platform's content guidelines during video calls.
  • Use a strong, unique password (12+ characters with letters, numbers, and symbols).
  • Be cautious of phishing attempts — Adda Live will never ask for your password or request a transfer via email or private message.

Penalties: permanent account ban; permanent device ban; serious cases will be referred to law enforcement.

OTHER PROHIBITED CONDUCT

Rumors & False Information

Adda Live is committed to building a trustworthy environment. Deliberately spreading false information that misleads users or damages the platform's reputation will be treated as a serious violation.

Violation: fabricating or distributing unverified rumors; falsified screen recordings; maliciously misrepresenting others or platform policies; coordinated fake reports.

Penalties: permanent account ban; all associated devices blacklisted.

Adult Content & Body Exposure

Adda Live is a social and entertainment platform, not an adult-services site. No sexual or suggestive material is allowed.

Violation: live sexual acts; sexual solicitation; nudity or sexual content, including exposure of sexual organs; deliberate emphasis on intimate body parts.

Penalties: content removal; repeated violations lead to feature restrictions (live streaming, video chat) and ultimately account suspension.

Fraud & Scamming

We aggressively act against any attempt to defraud users of gifts, coins, or personal data under the guise of Adda Live.

Violation: impersonating staff; emotional or financial scamming; phishing links; misleading payment requests.

Penalties: account ban; monetary fine equal to the defrauded amount where applicable; device blacklisting for serious cases.

Account Security & Hacking

Every user's account is their own. Unauthorized access or malicious intrusion is forbidden.

Violation: sharing, trading, or using another user's login details without consent.

Penalties: account ban; device blacklisting; possible fine.

Circumvention & Cheating

To preserve fairness, bypassing our security measures is strictly prohibited.

Violation: use of VMs, emulators, VPNs, proxies, or other tools to evade face verification or IP/geolocation restrictions.

Penalties: account ban; device blacklisting; possible fine.

Multiple Accounts

Creating multiple accounts to boost metrics, evade sanctions, or farm gifts is not allowed.

Violation: repeated registration; switching accounts to avoid penalties; mass account farming.

Penalties: related new accounts banned for face verification.

Inappropriate Profile Images

Profiles must reflect the real user. Stolen, AI-generated, or any inappropriate image is not allowed.

Violation: using someone else's photo; frequently uploading vulgar or violent avatars; implying false identity.

Penalties: profile image removal; repeated violations result in account suspension.

Other Misconduct

Including but not limited to:

  • Harassment, hate speech, and discrimination
  • Violence, gore, and animal abuse
  • Self-harm and suicide
  • Illegal transactions and prohibited items
  • Intellectual property and copyright violations
  • Spam, automation, and data scraping

Penalties: content removal; account ban or device ban for severe cases.

ENFORCEMENT & APPEALS

Monitoring: we employ automated detection and human review to identify and act on violations.

Sanctions: measures range from content takedown and feature restrictions to permanent account/device bans and financial penalties, and, for confirmed CSAE, reporting to NCMEC's CyberTipline and referral to law enforcement.

Appeals: if you believe a penalty was issued in error or wish to request leniency, you may submit an appeal through in-app customer support within 3 business days. Please include all relevant evidence and a clear explanation. In some cases, users may request reinstatement by submitting a written commitment not to violate the guidelines again. Adda Live will review all appeals and issue a decision within 10 business days. Accounts banned for confirmed CSAE violations are not eligible for appeal.

Notice Period: changes take effect immediately upon posting. We recommend reviewing this page often. Continued use of Adda Live after updates constitutes acceptance of the new terms.

Final Interpretation: PixelLab Ltd reserves the exclusive right to interpret these guidelines. In case of dispute, we may supplement or adjust provisions in line with legal requirements and operational needs, and issue a binding decision.

SAFETY GUIDE

Protect Yourself Online

  • Keep conversations on Adda Live — until you fully trust someone, keep calls and messages inside the app. Be cautious if they push to move to other platforms.
  • Protect your personal information — do not share your address, ID/passport numbers, verification codes/OTP, banking or crypto details, work location, daily routines, or travel plans with strangers.
  • Reject off-app transfers or gifts — decline any requests for money, gift exchanges, or cryptocurrency. Report such requests immediately.
  • Use a strong, unique password — at least 12 characters with letters, numbers, and symbols. Never reuse passwords across platforms.
  • Beware of phishing attacks — Adda Live will never ask for your password or money via email or private message. Always verify links before clicking.

Use Safety Tools

Report: tap the report icon on any profile, livestream, message, or post, select "Report", confirm, and submit. Our team reviews reports and takes action promptly.

Prevent Image & Video Misuse

  • Adda Live uses multi-layer anti-recording technology to help block screen capture during live interactions.
  • If you suspect someone has recorded or shared your content without consent: end the interaction immediately, report the user via Adda Live, and contact local law enforcement if applicable.
  • Non-consensual sharing of intimate content is strictly prohibited and may be illegal.
