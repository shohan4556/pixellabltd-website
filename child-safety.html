Child Safety Standards

Effective Date: 12th August, 2026

This page applies to Adda - Live Stream & Chat ("Adda Live", "the Service"), developed and operated by PixelLab Ltd. ("we", "us", "our").

OUR ZERO-TOLERANCE POLICY

Adda Live prohibits Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) in any form. We do not allow content that sexualizes, exploits, or endangers minors on our platform. This policy applies to every feature of the Service, including live streaming, chat, messaging, profiles, and any other user-generated content.

AGE RESTRICTIONS

  • Users must be at least 18 years old to access or use Adda Live.
  • Any attempt to falsify age in order to access the Service is strictly prohibited. Accounts found to have done so are suspended or permanently deleted upon discovery.
  • We use a combination of human moderation and automated monitoring systems to detect and act on underage access and any form of Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE). Attempts to bypass these safeguards are treated as a serious violation.

EXPLICITLY PROHIBITED ACTIVITIES

1. Sexualization of Minors and CSAE

  • Creation, sharing, or distribution of any CSAE content is strictly prohibited.
  • No fetishization of minors, including related discussions, suggestive performances, or provocative attire.
  • Depictions of child nudity or sexualization in any form, including drawings or animations, are not allowed.

2. Harmful or Dangerous Acts Involving Minors

  • No physical violence against minors.
  • No psychological abuse, coercion, or manipulation of minors.
  • No depiction or encouragement of dangerous activities involving minors.
  • No promotion or depiction of neglect, trafficking, or the sale of minors.

3. Minor Representation on the Platform

  • Minors are not permitted to appear in livestreams, videos, or any content on the platform.
  • No creation or management of accounts on behalf of minors.
  • No falsifying age to access the platform.

ENFORCEMENT

Any user who violates this Child Safety Standards policy will have the offending content immediately removed and their account permanently banned. Violations are also reported to the appropriate authorities, including the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) CyberTipline, and may result in referral to law enforcement.

HOW TO REPORT

If you encounter content or behavior that endangers a minor, use the in-app report tool available on any profile, stream, or message, or contact us directly using the details below. Reports are reviewed promptly by our Trust & Safety team.
