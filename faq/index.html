16 Guti & Sholo Guti

What is 16 Guti?

16 Guti (ষোল গুটি, Sholo Guti) is a traditional two-player strategy board game from South Asia. Each player has 16 beads on a lined board and captures the other player’s beads by jumping over them. It is also called 16 Goti, 16 Koti, Bagh Bakri, Baghchal, Bara Tehni and Sixteen Soldiers.

How do you play 16 Guti?

Each player starts with 16 beads. Players take turns moving one bead along the lines of the board. Jumping over an opponent’s bead captures it. The first player to capture all of the opponent’s beads wins.

Where can I play Sholo Guti online?

PixelLab Ltd. makes two Android apps for playing Sholo Guti online against real players: 16 Guti Game and 16 Guti: Sholo Guti Pro. Both are free to download on Google Play.

Can I play 16 Guti offline?

Yes. Both 16 Guti Game and Sholo Guti Pro have a local 2-player mode on one device and a single-player mode against the computer, and neither needs an internet connection.

What is the difference between 16 Guti Game and Sholo Guti Pro?

16 Guti Game is the classic version with online, local 2-player and single-player modes. 16 Guti: Sholo Guti Pro is the premium version with four game modes, VIP rooms, ranked online play and a computer bot with Easy, Medium and Hard levels.
