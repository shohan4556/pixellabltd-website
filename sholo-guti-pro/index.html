Sholo Guti, the way it’s played at home

Sholo Guti (ষোল গুটি, ১৬ গুটি) is one of the most popular board games in Bangladesh, India and Pakistan. In Bangladesh it’s also called গুটি খেলা or বাঘ বন্দি খেলা (Bagh Bondi). Elsewhere people know it as Bagh Bakri, Bara Tehni, 16 Kati, Tigers and Goats, or Sixteen Soldiers.

Two players hop over each other’s pieces to capture them. Capture all of the other player’s beads and you win.
